ABOUT AHMAD

Ahmad Malak Homsi began his career in sales

01 / ABOUT

Ahmad Malak Homsi began his career in book fair sales in 2016, working directly with customers. He went on to supervise and manage sales at Dar Al-Kitab Al-Natiq in Oman and represented the publisher in television interviews at regional book fairs.

Homsi worked in public relations and digital advertising before joining Beisat as Marketing Manager in Muscat in November 2022. He became CEO in September 2024. He is also a partner at Tornado Projects & Services, which owns the Beisat brand, and holds a stake in OSH Group UK.

In negotiations, Homsi focuses on understanding the other party’s needs and agreeing on practical next steps.

BEISAT CASE STUDY

Beisat: freeing up cash to refresh inventory

Since becoming CEO, Homsi has worked with the Beisat team to refresh inventory, control costs and open new sales channels.

02 / BEISAT

Inside Beisat’s warehouse in Oman.

A NEW SALES CHANNEL · AUGUST 2025

Homsi helped launch container-based wholesale and negotiated Beisat’s first deal for the Iraqi market. Beisat’s B2B team introduced the customer, who met with Homsi at the company’s Muscat office.

01

The challenge

Part of the working capital was tied up in older inventory, while the business needed cash for fresh products.

02

The decision

Clear part of the older inventory, accepting losses on some items, and reinvest the cash in new stock.

03

Execution

Purchasing, payment, shipping, and preparation took approximately three months before the first new stock was ready to sell.

04

The outcome

Beisat maintained sales through the transition. Sales rose once the new stock became available.
